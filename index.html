ভালোবাসি bhalobashi — I love you

Dear [Name],

There is a particular hush that falls over the city just before the rain — the sky turns the colour of old brass, the koel goes quiet, and everyone on the street looks up at once. I think of you in that hush. I think of you the way the first drop feels on skin that has been waiting for it all summer.

You have become woven into the small, ordinary hours of my days — the second cup of cha that's really just an excuse to sit a little longer, the shiuli flowers I've noticed have started falling again, the songs that used to be background noise and are now unmistakably ours. I did not plan to love you this completely. It happened the way the monsoon happens — slowly, and then all at once.

So this letter is not really news. You already know. But some things deserve to be written down anyway, in ink, on paper that will yellow and soften with time — the way I hope we do. However far the tide carries us, know that some part of me is always walking home to you.

Forever yours,

Ahnaf
